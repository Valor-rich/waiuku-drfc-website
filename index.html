01 / WHO WE ARE

ROOTED IN
OUR COMMUNITY.

Waiuku District Rugby Football Club is more than a place to play. It’s a place to belong.

We’re proud to bring people together through rugby — players, whānau, volunteers, supporters and local businesses. Whether you’re pulling on the boots, lending a hand or cheering from the sideline, there’s a place for you here.
